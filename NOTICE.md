# Notices and Acknowledgements

## WoWAnalyzer

FellowshipAnalyzer is based on, and is essentially a C# / Blazor WebAssembly
clone of, [WoWAnalyzer](https://github.com/WoWAnalyzer/WoWAnalyzer) adapted
for the online RPG [Fellowship](https://www.fellowship.gg/).

Many of the analysis patterns, module structures, guide and statistics
concepts, normalizer pipeline ideas, and the overall approach to
event-driven combat log analysis used in this project are derived from
WoWAnalyzer. Specific implementations have been ported from TypeScript and
React to modern C# and Blazor, and adapted to take advantage of features
such as source generators and strong compile-time analyzer registration,
but the design owes a substantial debt to the WoWAnalyzer contributors and
their many years of work.

- Upstream project: <https://github.com/WoWAnalyzer/WoWAnalyzer>

In recognition of that lineage, FellowshipAnalyzer is also distributed
under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE), the same
license as WoWAnalyzer.

A heartfelt thank you to the WoWAnalyzer maintainers and contributors for
building and openly sharing the project that made FellowshipAnalyzer
possible.
